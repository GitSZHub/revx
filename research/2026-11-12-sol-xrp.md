# Current strategy on SOL-EUR and XRP-EUR, separate EUR 400 pots

Script: `research/altcoin_backtest.py`. Settings unchanged from `config.toml` (50 EUR tranche, max 5 lots per pair,
32h breakout pause, floor +3%, trail 3 x ATR). Each pair is backtested alone with its own EUR 400.
Pot currency is EUR (the bot trades -EUR pairs); the request said USD.

## Window

Asked for January. Not possible: Revolut X candle history starts 2025-11-06 and the indicators need about 3 months
(90-day vol percentile), so every pair's first usable candle is **2026-02-07**. All results are 2026-02-07 to 2026-11-12 (278 days).

## Reading it

- SOL and XRP behave like BTC and ETH: about +10% each, in the same range. Nothing here says they are better or worse.
- The strategy beat holding by a lot on XRP (-22% held) and SOL (+2% held), lost to holding on BTC.
- SOL realized the most (+91) but 5 lots are still open and underwater, which is why equity is only +41.7.
  Its open lots are the main risk in this result.
- Only 50 EUR per lot and at most 5 lots per pair, so most of each pot sits as cash (up to EUR 405 on ETH).
  The percentages are on the whole 400.
- Backtest buys fill at the candle close, so real results will probably be somewhat worse (see CLAUDE.md).
- One 9-month window in a mostly falling market. Not enough to change the pair list on its own.
